Dashboard

Semester progress at a glance

The overview sidebar shows you exactly where you stand in each course. See which weeks have unfinished work, which types of assignments remain, and how much of the semester you have completed — all without leaving the page. Each course pill also shows its own progress bar so you can track every subject at a glance.

  • Per-week completion stats with visual progress bars
  • Item type breakdowns (Quizzes, Assignments, Files, Pages)
  • Course-wide totals: done, unfinished, and unknown items
  • Per-course progress bars in the navigation pills
Timetable

Your schedule, always accessible

A fully editable weekly timetable that lives in your browser. Add, edit, or remove subjects and periods. The timetable highlights the current period and today's column so you always know what is next.

  • Edit subjects inline or with a dedicated editor
  • Current period and today highlighting
  • Responsive design with mobile-friendly modal editing
  • Data stored locally — no server uploads

Getting started

Three simple steps to start browsing.

1

Generate an Access Card

Open your Canvas account settings and create a new API access token. This is your personal Access Card.

2

Paste it into VinFocus

Copy your Access Card and paste it into the VinFocus setup wizard. The card is stored only in your browser.

3

Start browsing

Your courses load automatically. Navigate weeks, search resources, and track your semester progress.
